Welcome to the Physics Repository

⚠️ Notice: This repository is currently a work in progress. Some features may not yet be fully available.

The Physics Repository is a publicly accessible platform for sharing, searching, and reusing research data from physics experiments. It serves researchers, students, and collaborating institutions.

It is managed by the Institute of Physics of the Czech Academy of Sciences (FZU) and technically operated by CESNET.

Data are organized into thematic communities corresponding to individual research groups or experiments. Access is managed by community administrators — some data are openly available, while others require membership.

For more information about management, operations, the technical platform, and how to request access, see the About page.
